# Torn market knowledge

Facts the advisor can rely on. Add your own observations here; every line is
sent to Gemini with the data. Keep it to things you've checked.

## Halloween (Trick or Treat), from Romarocky's 2025 guide
- Runs about 25 October to 1 November. Each player gets 168 hours from their chosen start time.
- You win treats by winning attacks (final hit only). Treats fill and upgrade a Halloween basket.
- The basket costs $35 at Sally's Sweet Shop and needs level 5. Without it you earn nothing.
- Scary clothing gives +10% treat chance; a scary weapon gives +10% when it lands the final hit.
- First-year players won't make money from the event; the goal is basket progress (Terrifying = 440 treats).
- Energy drinks and boosters are cheaper right after Christmas and more expensive in September and October.
- Revitalize weapons are also pricier just before Halloween.
- Basket exchanges give candy, alcohol, energy drinks and boosters, so those items get extra supply after the event.

## Observed price patterns
- Bag of Humbugs (Candy, Christmas-only supply, consumable): circulation jumps once a year at Christmas,
  then falls all year as people eat them. Price dips just after Christmas and climbs through the year,
  peaking in November–December. In October 2026 it was about $700–745k, near its yearly high.
  Pattern: buy in January, sell in November–December.

## General rules
- Items sold in city shops or abroad have endless supply; hoarding them doesn't work.
- Event-only items that get consumed are the best hoard candidates.
- Item market listings have a fee when they sell.
