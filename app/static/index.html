Torn Companion

Your timers, plans, notes and faction in one calm place. Sign in with your Torn API key.

How do I get a key?
  1. In Torn, open Settings → API Keys.
  2. Create a new key with Limited Access and name it something like "Companion".
  3. Copy the 16-character key and paste it above.
What happens with your key
Data storageYour profile, bars, money, inventory, timers, events and fights, plus your journal, checklists, reminders, board and player tags, are stored until you delete your account. Market prices are stored for everyone.
Data sharingOnly you can see your data. Anything you add to your faction's space is visible to your faction's members who use this dashboard. Nothing is sold. The person running this server can access its database.
Purpose of useYour own dashboard: timers and reminders, planning, notes, your faction and trading advice.
Key storage & sharingStored encrypted on this server. Used only to fetch your own data while you have the dashboard open, and every 10 minutes in the background only if you turn on notifications. Never shared. Deleted with your account.
Key access levelLimited Access
Advisor (optional)Only if you add a Gemini key: your game data (never your Torn key) is sent to Google Gemini when you ask for advice.
